Note: The version information described in the Recommended Data Format section of the presentation slides included in this folder is provided for guidance only. Automotive SBOM does not prescribe any specific SBOM document format or version. Supported examples include SPDX 2.x/3.x and CycloneDX 1.4/1.5/1.6/1.7. Future versions of these formats may also be used, provided that the required data fields specified in Chapter 3.2 of the Automotive SBOM Specification can be represented.
